About Me

I'm Hrishikesh Keswani, a Machine Learning Engineer based in Boston with over 2 years of experience building scalable AI and data-driven systems. I specialize in end-to-end machine learning pipelines, with a strong focus on LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and production ML systems.

My strengths lie at the intersection of model development and real-world deployment—from fine-tuning and inference optimization to distributed training, monitoring, and cloud-native deployment on AWS, Docker, and Kubernetes. Outside of work, I'm passionate about building AI products and experimenting with new LLM architectures.
